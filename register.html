— What follows

After you write to the atelier.

Every enquiry is read by a senior member of the trade desk before it is routed — never a bot, never a queue.

  • 01

    A reply within two working days

    Including a senior contact, suggested next steps, and a window of dates for a deeper conversation.

  • 02

    A curated selection of surfaces

    Composed against your brief — material, palette, format, finish — with technical sheets and CAD files attached.

  • 03

    Samples to the studio

    Posted at our cost, packed in linen — A5 samples for shortlisted references, A3 for finalists.

  • 04

    An invitation to Casalgrande

    For projects above 500 m², we host studios at the atelier — to see the slabs pressed, polished and selected by hand.
